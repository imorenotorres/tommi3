## Summary

TELL-ME (Towards European Language Learning for MEdical professionals) is a European project focused on developing language learning resources and tools for medical professionals working across European countries. The project addresses the linguistic challenges faced by healthcare workers in multilingual environments, combining expertise in computational linguistics, translation technology, and medical communication.

The consortium brought together universities with expertise in natural language processing and translation technology (University of Wolverhampton, Universitat des Saarlandes, Universidad de Malaga) with healthcare institutions (Universitatsmedizin Mannheim, Hospital Pascual, NHS West Midlands Workforce Deanery) to create practical language learning solutions for the medical sector.

**Keywords:** language learning, medical professionals, multilingualism, translation technology, healthcare communication, NLP, European mobility
