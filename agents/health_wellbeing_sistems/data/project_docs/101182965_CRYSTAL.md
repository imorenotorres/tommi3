**Period:** 2024-11-01 — 2028-10-31
**Status:** Active

**Website:** https://cordis.europa.eu/project/id/101182965
**Project website:** https://project-crystal.eu/

## Summary

CRYSTAL develops advanced conversational models and systems that implement reliable, friendly, and efficient human-machine interactions for two main application areas: emotional support to vulnerable populations (such as ageing adults or people experiencing anxiety or depression) and customer assistance in call centres. The project builds on the results of the MENHIR project (Grant 823907) and proposes novel learning strategies for domain adaptation, explores Large Language Models (LLMs) and prompt engineering methodologies, user state estimation through behavioural models, and dialogue management guided by stakeholder goals. The project emphasises ethical compliance, AI robustness, and knowledge exchange through staff secondments.

**Keywords:** Conversational AI, Emotional Support, Ageing Well, Large Language Models, Mental Health, Customer Assistance, Dialogue Systems, Prompt Engineering
