# STRATEGY-CKD: System omics to unravel the gut-kidney axis in Chronic Kidney Disease

**Grant ID:** 860329
**Funder:** European Commission
**Programme:** H2020 - Marie Sklodowska-Curie Actions
**Funding scheme:** MSCA-ITN
**Period:** 2020-02-01 — 2024-07-31
**Status:** CLOSED
**Total cost:** 3,363,591.24 (funded: 3,363,591.24) EUR
**Website:** https://cordis.europa.eu/project/id/860329

## Summary

This initiative creates a training programme with focus on the gut-kidney axis and the role of the intestinal microbiome in the genesis and evolution of CVD in CKD. The program trains researchers in molecular studies, omics approaches, bioinformatics, and systems biology to advance understanding of kidney disease complications.

**Keywords:** Chronic kidney disease, cardiovascular disease, gut-kidney axis, omics
