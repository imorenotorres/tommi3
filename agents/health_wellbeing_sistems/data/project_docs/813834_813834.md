**Website:** https://cordis.europa.eu/project/id/813834

## Summary

The research team investigated whether the pancreas's unique acidic environment facilitates cancer initiation and progression. The project aimed to map pancreatic pH landscapes, characterize how acidic microenvironments influence disease development, and design targeted treatment approaches. The work combined expertise in ion transport, oncology, imaging, bioinformatics, and antibody technology to advance understanding of pancreatic ductal adenocarcinoma (PDAC).

**Keywords:** Pancreatic cancer, pancreas, acid-base physiology, microenvironment, transportome, cell-cell signalling, imaging, bioinformatics, antibody technology
