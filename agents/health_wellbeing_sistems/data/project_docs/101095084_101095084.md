# ENDOTARGET: Systemic Endotoxemia as the driver of chronic inflammation - Biomarkers and novel therapeutic targets for Arthritis

**Programme:** HORIZON.2.1 - Health

**Status:** ACTIVE
**Total cost:** 6,756,911.24 (funded: 6,756,911.24) EUR
**Website:** https://cordis.europa.eu/project/id/101095084

## Summary

The project investigates chronic systemic inflammation from intestinal microbiota as a driver transitioning from health to disease, focusing on osteoarthritis, rheumatoid arthritis, and spondylarthritis. Researchers will examine gut microbiota, intestinal permeability, and endotoxemia relationships using omics analyses, clinical studies, mechanistic investigations with tissue cultures and organ-on-chip models, and dietary interventions.

**Keywords:** Chronic inflammation, microbiota, systemic endotoxemia, intestinal permeability, rheumatoid arthritis, osteoarthritis, spondylarthritis
