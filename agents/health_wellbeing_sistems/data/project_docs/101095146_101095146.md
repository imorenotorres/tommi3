**Website:** https://cordis.europa.eu/project/id/101095146

## Summary

The initiative addresses the absence of validated pharmacodynamic biomarkers for chronic kidney disease treatment. It aims to validate and implement biomarkers as clinical tools for personalized medicine, establish stakeholder engagement networks, and develop implementation roadmaps. The project will enable clinicians to match patients with optimal therapeutics, reducing disease progression and improving cost-efficiency while bridging the gap between biomarker research and clinical practice.

**Keywords:** Chronic Kidney Disease, Personalized Medicine, Pharmacodynamic Biomarkers, Clinical Implementation
