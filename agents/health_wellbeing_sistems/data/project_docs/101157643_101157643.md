## Summary

Climate change poses significant threats to human health and well-being. The increasing frequency of extreme events like floods, wildfires, and heatwaves has far-reaching consequences for local communities. AURORA aims to enhance resilience against health risks by developing tools to monitor climate stressors, create models, generate forecasts, identify vulnerabilities, and issue early warnings with AI-driven technology recommending adaptive and nature-based solutions.

**Keywords:** Climate change and health impacts, Risks, Vulnerabilities, Epidemiological modelling, Nature-based solutions
