# Solve-RD: Solving the unsolved Rare Diseases

**Grant ID:** 779257
**Funder:** European Commission
**Programme:** H2020 - SOCIETAL CHALLENGES - Health, demographic change and well-being
**Funding scheme:** RIA (Research and Innovation action)
**Period:** 2018-01-01 — 2024-03-31
**Status:** Project closed
**Total cost:** 15,361,621.25 (funded: 15,361,621.25) EUR
**Website:** https://cordis.europa.eu/project/id/779257

## Summary

The initiative aims to solve large numbers of rare diseases lacking identified molecular causes through advanced omics approaches and improve diagnostics via a "genetic knowledge web." Using a "beyond the exome" strategy, the consortium includes clinicians, geneticists, translational researchers, research infrastructures, patient organizations, and omics experts. The project pursues seven implementation steps from phenotype collection through functional analysis to therapy development, anticipating diagnostic yield increases of 3-5% across 19,000 unsolved cases and solving over 2,000 cases.

**Keywords:** European Reference Networks, Solving the Unsolved, Expert-driven and data-driven approaches
