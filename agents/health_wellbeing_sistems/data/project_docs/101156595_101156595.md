**Grant ID:** 101156595

**Funding scheme:** HORIZON-COFUND (Cofund Actions)
**Period:** 2024-09-01 — 2031-08-31
**Status:** Active
**Total cost:** 145,830,619.47 (funded: 56,317,412.61) EUR
**Website:** https://cordis.europa.eu/project/id/101156595

## Summary

The European Rare Diseases Research Alliance (ERDERA) aims to improve the health and well-being of the 30 million people living with a rare disease in Europe, by making Europe a world leader in Rare Disease (RD) research and innovation, to support concrete health benefits to rare disease patients, through better prevention, diagnosis and treatment.

**Keywords:** translational research, diagnostics, therapy development, ATMPs, data, education, patients empowerment, public-private collaboration, federated analysis, regulatory compliance, clinical trials
