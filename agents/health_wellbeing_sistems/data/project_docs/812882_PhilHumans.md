# PhilHumans: Personal Health Interfaces Leveraging Human-Machine Natural Interactions

**Grant ID:** 812882
**Funder:** European Commission
**Programme:** H2020 — Marie Sklodowska-Curie Innovative Training Networks (MSCA-ITN)
**Period:** 2019-01-01 — 2023-10-31
**Status:** Closed
**Total cost:** 2135436 (funded: 2135436) EUR
**Website:** https://cordis.europa.eu/project/id/812882

## Summary

The PhilHumans project trains emerging researchers in AI innovation focused on healthcare device interfaces. It provides Marie Sklodowska-Curie PhD Fellowships to eight Early Stage Researchers pursuing interdisciplinary work across cognitive computing, machine learning, natural language processing, and computer vision applied to maternal/child health, wellness, and personal care domains. The project combines speech and text processing with advanced computer vision for conversational health interfaces, emotion detection, and social interaction in healthcare settings.

**Keywords:** Personal Health Care, Human-Machine Interaction, Artificial Intelligence, Cognitive Computing, Deep Learning, Natural Language Processing, Natural Language Generation, Computer Vision, Maternal Health
