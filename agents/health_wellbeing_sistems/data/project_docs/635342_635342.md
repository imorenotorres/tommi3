# MoTriColor: Molecularly guided trials with specific treatment strategies in patients with advanced newly molecular defined subtypes of colorectal cancer

**Grant ID:** 635342
**Funder:** European Commission
**Programme:** H2020 - SOCIETAL CHALLENGES - Health, demographic change and well-being
**Funding scheme:** RIA (Research and Innovation action)
**Period:** 2015-10-01 — 2020-11-30
**Status:** Project closed
**Total cost:** 4,948,742.50 (funded: 4,948,742.50) EUR
**Website:** https://cordis.europa.eu/project/id/635342

## Summary

The project addresses colorectal cancer as a heterogeneous disease with distinct molecular subtypes requiring tailored therapeutic approaches. Researchers developed molecular diagnostic assays identifying gene expression profiles with different prognoses and drug responses. The initiative proposes three phase II studies testing targeted therapies: chemotherapy combined with TGF-betaR inhibitor for C-type signatures, vinorelbine for BRAF-mutant-like cases, and anti-PD-L1 immunotherapy with bevacizumab for MSI-like signatures. Approximately 40,000 European CRC patients could potentially benefit.

**Keywords:** colorectal cancer, molecular subtypes, immunotherapy, precision medicine, gene expression profiling, targeted therapy
