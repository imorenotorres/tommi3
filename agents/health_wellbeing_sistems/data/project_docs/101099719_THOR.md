# THOR: Building vascular networks and Blood-Brain-Barriers through a Biomimetic manufacturing Technology for the fabrication of Human tissues and ORgans

**Grant ID:** 101099719
**Funder:** European Innovation Council (EIC)
**Programme:** Horizon Europe — EIC Pathfinder
**Period:** 2023-01-01 — 2026-12-31
**Status:** Active
**Total cost:** 3994150 (funded: 3994150) EUR
**Website:** https://cordis.europa.eu/project/id/101099719

## Summary

The THOR project aims to develop revolutionary tissue engineering technology producing human tissues for organ repair or replacement. Patient-tailored tissues will be fabricated by pools of bioinspired mini-robots in fully automated production plants, using breakthrough incremental technologies: self-assembling molecules inspired by the extracellular matrix and self-assembling solid and hollow polymeric fibres. The project targets creating well-vascularised, fully functioning tissues including vascular networks and Blood-Brain-Barriers for clinical applications. This approach combines advanced biomaterial science, robotics, and AI to address the critical shortage of donor organs and tissues.

**Keywords:** Tissue Engineering, Biomimetic Manufacturing, Vascular Networks, Blood-Brain-Barrier, Bioinspired Robotics, Self-Assembly, Regenerative Medicine, Organ Fabrication
