**Period:** 2019-02-01 — 2024-01-31

**Website:** https://cordis.europa.eu/project/id/823907
**Project website:** https://menhir-project.eu/

## Summary

The MENHIR project researches and develops conversational technologies to promote mental health and assist people with mild depression and anxiety manage their conditions. The chatbot technology provides around-the-clock support, personalised motivation, useful resources, and coping strategies. It processes emotional and mood patterns from voice recordings and prompts users with supportive messages and motivational reminders to follow action plans. The project combines expertise in information and communication technologies, psychology and cognitive science, conversational systems, and cross-modal emotion analysis.

**Keywords:** Mental Health, Depression, Anxiety, Conversational AI, Chatbots, Dialogue Systems, Acoustic Analysis, Emotion Recognition, Wellbeing, NLP
