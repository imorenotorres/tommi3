# BRECISE: Biomarker Research and Evaluation for Clinical Implementation and Supporting Systems Enhancement

**Grant ID:** 101194784
**Funder:** European Commission
**Programme:** HORIZON.2.1 - Health (Innovative Health Initiative)
**Funding scheme:** HORIZON-JU-RIA (HORIZON JU Research and Innovation Actions)
**Period:** 2025-01-01 — 2029-12-31
**Status:** Active
**Total cost:** 22,872,818.75 (funded: 13,615,935.25) EUR
**Website:** https://cordis.europa.eu/project/id/101194784

## Summary

BRECISE is a Public-Private Partnership Project co-funded by the Innovative Health Initiative aiming at creating a collaborative ecosystem to accelerate the clinical validation of Next-Generation Sequencing (NGS)-based, multi-modality Artificial Intelligence (AI) oncology biomarkers and related technologies.

**Keywords:** Prostate Cancer, Bladder Cancer, Oncology, Personalized Oncology, Sequencing, NGS
