**Grant ID:** 101194784
**Funder:** Innovative Health Initiative (IHI) Joint Undertaking
**Programme:** Horizon Europe — Innovative Health Initiative
**Period:** 2025-01-01 — 2029-12-31
**Status:** Active
**Total cost:** 22872819 (funded: 13615935) EUR
**Website:** https://cordis.europa.eu/project/id/101194784

## Summary

The BRECISE project advances precision medicine in oncology through clinically validated biomarkers. It aims to enhance biomarker-driven approaches for patient risk stratification, disease progression prediction, and treatment response assessment. The project develops NGS-based, multi-modality AI oncology biomarkers targeting prostate and bladder cancers. Expected outcomes include optimised personalised cancer care and strengthened competitiveness of European health industries. The Fundacion para la Investigacion de Malaga en Biomedicina y Salud (FIMABIS), the biomedical research foundation closely affiliated with the Universidad de Malaga, participates as a third-party research organisation.

**Keywords:** Prostate Cancer, Bladder Cancer, Oncology, Precision Medicine, Biomarkers, NGS, AI Diagnostics, Personalised Oncology
