# NextGEM: Next Generation Integrated Sensing and Analytical System for Monitoring and Assessing Radiofrequency Electromagnetic Field Exposure and Health

**Grant ID:** 101057527
**Funder:** European Commission
**Programme:** Horizon Europe — Health (Environmental and Social Health Determinants)
**Period:** 2022-07-01 — 2026-06-30
**Status:** Active
**Total cost:** 7559040 (funded: 7559039) EUR
**Website:** https://cordis.europa.eu/project/id/101057527
**Project website:** https://www.nextgem.eu/

## Summary

The NextGEM project generates knowledge on electromagnetic field (EMF) exposure across residential, public, and occupational environments. It designs a new framework for the generation of health-relevant scientific knowledge and data on new scenarios of exposure to EMF in multiple frequency bands, with an overall aim to provide a healthy living and working environment under safe EMF exposure conditions. The project creates validated risk assessment tools and establishes the NextGEM Innovation and Knowledge Hub (NIKH) to standardise how European regulatory authorities store and evaluate outcomes while providing accessible FAIR data. NextGEM addresses the health implications of 5G and next-generation telecommunications technologies.

**Keywords:** Electromagnetic Fields, Radiofrequency Exposure, Health Monitoring, Risk Assessment, Environmental Health, 5G, Public Health, Occupational Safety
