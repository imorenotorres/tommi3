# halt-RONIN: Discovering chronic inflammation biomarkers that define key stages in the Healthy-to-NASH transition to inform early prevention and treatment strategies

**Grant ID:** 101095679
**Funder:** European Commission
**Programme:** HORIZON.2.1 - Health
**Funding scheme:** HORIZON-RIA
**Period:** 2022-12-01 — 2026-11-30
**Status:** ACTIVE
**Total cost:** 6,721,927.51 (funded: 6,721,927.26) EUR
**Website:** https://cordis.europa.eu/project/id/101095679

## Summary

The project aims to uncover the early triggers of disease initiation and complex mechanistic drivers of disease progression in NAFLD through systems biology approaches. It combines experimental data from advanced models with human cohort data and machine learning to discover biomarkers and molecular targets specific to each disease stage, ultimately helping healthcare professionals diagnose and establish prevention guidelines.

**Keywords:** NAFLD, NASH, chronic inflammation, disease transition, biomarkers, molecular mechanisms, blueprint, prevention, personalized, intervention, prediction, progression
