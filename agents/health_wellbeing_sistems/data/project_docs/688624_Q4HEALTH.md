# Q4HEALTH: Quality of Service and prioritisation for emergency services in the LTE RAN stack

**Grant ID:** 688624
**Funder:** European Commission
**Programme:** H2020 — ICT (Innovation Action)
**Period:** 2016-01-01 — 2018-02-28
**Status:** Closed
**Total cost:** 855037 (funded: 728176) EUR
**Website:** https://cordis.europa.eu/project/id/688624

## Summary

The Q4HEALTH project focused on optimising real-time video performance for emergency medical responders using LTE networks. The project involved experiments across FIRE platforms examining wearable video delivery for first responders, scheduling algorithms, QoS negotiation capabilities, and indoor service availability. It addressed network coordination between geographically dispersed entities, explored scheduling strategies for emergency video transmission, and investigated SDN techniques plus seamless mobility mechanisms to enable remote medical diagnosis (telemedicine). Twenty KPIs were established and measured throughout implementation.

**Keywords:** Telemedicine, Emergency Services, LTE, Real-time Video, Wearable Devices, Quality of Service, Remote Diagnosis
