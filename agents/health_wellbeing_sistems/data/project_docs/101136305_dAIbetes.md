# dAIbetes: Federated virtual twins for privacy-preserving personalised outcome prediction of type 2 diabetes treatment

## Summary

The dAIbetes project develops federated virtual twin models for personalised prediction of treatment outcomes in type 2 diabetes, which afflicts 1 in 10 adults worldwide and causes annual expenditures of approximately 893 billion EUR. While healthcare providers are becoming increasingly effective at targeting diabetes risk factors (e.g., diet or exercises), no guidelines as to the expected outcome for a given treatment for a specific patient exist. The federated dAIbetes technology harmonises existing data of approximately 800,000 type 2 diabetes patients from 4 cohorts distributed across the globe, and learns prognostic virtual twin models using federated learning — a privacy-preserving method where data remains behind institutional firewalls. The models are validated for clinical relevance and applied in clinical practice through dedicated software. The project aims to demonstrate that personalised predictions have a prediction error at least 10% lower than population average-based models. This federated virtual twin technology acts as a blueprint for other complex diseases.

**Keywords:** Federated Learning, Virtual Twins, Artificial Intelligence, Type 2 Diabetes, Precision Medicine, Privacy-Preserving AI, Personalised Treatment
