# EU-RESPONSE: European Research and Preparedness Network for Pandemics and Emerging Infectious Diseases

**Grant ID:** 101015736
**Funder:** European Commission
**Programme:** H2020 - SOCIETAL CHALLENGES - Health, demographic change and well-being
**Funding scheme:** RIA (Research and Innovation Action)
**Period:** 2020-07-01 — 2026-06-30
**Status:** Active
**Total cost:** 16,055,304.10 (funded: 15,719,450.00) EUR
**Website:** https://cordis.europa.eu/project/id/101015736

## Summary

This 5-year initiative establishes a multinational adaptive European COVID-19 and emerging infectious diseases trial network. Building on existing initiatives like the DisCoVeRy trial, the project enables European expansion and creates a COVID-19 adaptive platform trial. It generates robust evidence for drug repurposing through a modular framework allowing hospitals to participate at preferred commitment levels.

**Keywords:** COVID-19, Platform trials network, Emerging infectious diseases
