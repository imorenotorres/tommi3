## Summary

The 3D Tune-In project brings together SME digital games developers, academic institutions, hearing aid manufacturers, and hearing communities to produce gaming applications for hearing aids. The project addresses the challenge that newer hearing aid functionalities remain underutilised, particularly among children and older adults. It exploits existing, overlooked or neglected hearing aid functionalities to improve quality of life through a novel 3D Tune-In toolkit based on participatory design and advances in 3D visual, audio, and haptic technologies. The toolkit enables the development of games that help users learn and fine-tune their hearing aids in an engaging, accessible way.

**Keywords:** Hearing Aids, Assistive Technology, Gaming, 3D Audio, Active Ageing, Social Inclusion, Gamification, Haptic Feedback
