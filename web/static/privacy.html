Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 June 2025

This privacy policy explains how the Responsible AI Research Explorer ("the Service"), operated within the UNINOVIS DATA FOR L.I.F.E. project, processes personal data in accordance with the General Data Protection Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR).

1. Data Controller

Universidad de Málaga (UMA)
UNINOVIS-UMA ICT Team
Avda. de Cervantes, 2 — 29071 Málaga, Spain

For data protection inquiries, contact: dpo@uma.es

2. What Data We Process

Data categoryExamplesLegal basis (GDPR)
User queries Text entered in the chat interface Art. 6(1)(f) — Legitimate interest (providing the research service)
Audit log Timestamp, pseudonymised user ID, query text, query classification, model used Art. 6(1)(c) — Legal obligation (EU AI Act transparency requirements)
Session data Temporary session identifier for conversation continuity Art. 6(1)(f) — Legitimate interest (service functionality)

We do not collect: names, email addresses, passwords, IP addresses (Matomo is configured to anonymise IPs), or any special category data.

3. Researcher Data Displayed by the Service

The Service displays information about researchers from UNINOVIS partner universities, including names, institutional affiliations, and publication metadata (titles, DOIs, co-authors, topics). This data is sourced from publicly available academic databases (OpenAlex) and manually curated records and is processed under Art. 6(1)(f) — legitimate interest in facilitating academic research collaboration.

Researchers who wish to review, correct, or request removal of their data may contact the Data Controller.

5. Data Retention

  • Session data: Deleted at the end of the browser session
  • Audit log: Retained for the duration of the UNINOVIS project (until 31 December 2028), then deleted or anonymised
  • Analytics data: Retained according to Matomo Cloud's retention policy (default: 24 months)
  • User queries are not stored beyond the audit log and the active session

6. Your Rights

Under the GDPR, you have the right to:

  • Access your personal data (Art. 15)
  • Rectify inaccurate data (Art. 16)
  • Erase your data ("right to be forgotten") (Art. 17)
  • Restrict processing (Art. 18)
  • Object to processing based on legitimate interest (Art. 21)
  • Data portability (Art. 20)
  • Lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority — in Spain: Agencia Española de Protección de Datos (AEPD)

To exercise any of these rights, contact the Data Controller at the address above.

7. AI Transparency

In accordance with the EU AI Act (Regulation 2024/1689, Article 50), we inform you that:

  • This Service uses an AI system (large language model) to classify queries and generate some responses
  • Not all responses are AI-generated — many are produced directly from the research database without AI involvement
  • Each response displays a reliability cue indicating its source:
    • Green — Data sourced directly from the knowledge base (no AI)
    • Yellow — AI commentary based on knowledge base data
    • Red — Fully AI-generated content
  • When enabled, a decision trace shows how each query was classified and routed

8. Cookies

This Service uses Matomo Analytics in cookieless mode. No tracking cookies are placed on your device. A temporary session identifier is used for conversation continuity and is not stored persistently.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes in the Service or legal requirements. The "last updated" date at the top will be revised accordingly.
