Welcome & Informed Consent

Thank you for participating in this study on AI transparency. It takes approximately 12-15 minutes.

Purpose: This study compares two approaches to AI transparency — Content Transparency and Procedural Transparency — to understand which better supports user trust calibration when interacting with AI agents.

Procedure: You will (1) answer a brief background survey, (2) interact with two AI agents by asking 2 predefined questions to each, rating each agent immediately after, and (3) indicate your overall preference.

Data: Your participant ID is randomly assigned. No personal data beyond your login is collected. Data will be used for research purposes only.

Voluntary participation: You may withdraw at any time by closing this page. Incomplete responses will not be used.

Contact: For questions, please contact the research team at the University of Málaga.
