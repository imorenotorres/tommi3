Research Production

Explore the research output of the UNINOVIS alliance in the field of Responsible AI. Our Excellence Hub provides a unique tool to navigate this knowledge: the Responsible AI Agent, an intelligent assistant that lets you search, query, and discover UNINOVIS research production through natural conversation.

The agent is trained on curated publications, projects, and expertise from across the alliance, enabling you to find relevant work, identify potential collaborators, and understand research trends — all in one place.

Authentication required — Access to the Responsible AI Agent is restricted to verified members of the UNINOVIS community. This is a distinctive feature of our Excellence Hubs: intelligent, personalised research tools available exclusively to identified users.
