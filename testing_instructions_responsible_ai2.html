Testing the Responsible AI Agent

Instructions for UNINOVIS Testers — TOMMI Transparent Agents Platform

What is TOMMI?

TOMMI is a platform for deploying and using AI Agents that answer questions based on curated data (publications, researchers, projects). Unlike generic chatbots, TOMMI agents show you where each answer comes from — whether it is based on verified data or generated by the language model — so you can judge how reliable the response is.

Each response includes a reliability badge:

  • Green (High) — most of the response comes from verified data
  • Yellow (Good) — a mix of verified data and model-generated content
  • Red (Poor) — mostly generated by the language model, use with caution

What are we testing?

You will test the "EH: Responsible AI (proj&pub)" agent. This agent is a research assistant that covers:

  • Publications on Responsible AI topics across all 8 UNINOVIS partner universities
  • Researchers and their areas of expertise
  • Research projects (EU-funded and others) involving UNINOVIS partners
  • Collaborations between partner universities
  • Gap analysis — identifying Responsible AI subtopics not yet studied

The agent draws its answers from a curated database (227 papers, 86 projects, researcher profiles). Publication data was obtained from the OpenAlex academic database, and project data from the CORDIS European projects database. National-level projects are not included in the system. Your role is to verify that the information is correct, complete, and properly sourced, particularly for your own university.

How to access the system

  1. Open the TOMMI server URL provided by the administrator in your browser.
  2. Log in with your credentials. If it is your first time, you will be asked to set a new password.
  3. After login, you will land on the Tester interface.
  4. Select "EH: Responsible AI (proj&pub)" from the agent dropdown in the sidebar.

How to report errors

When you find an incorrect, incomplete, or misleading response:

  1. Click the thumbs-down button below the response.
  2. Select the error type (e.g., "3.1 Missing information", "3.2 Wrong information").
  3. Select the severity (Minor / Major / Critical).
  4. Write a brief note explaining what is wrong and what the correct answer should be.
  5. Click Send.

Your report is saved automatically and will be reviewed by the development team.

Offline data review (Excel)

In addition to testing the agent interactively, you can download the raw data as Excel files to review offline. This is particularly useful for systematically checking all researchers, publications, or projects for your university.

How to download

Ask your TOMMI administrator to provide the Excel files, or if you have access to the Agent management panel, download them directly from the Export data column. Three files are available per agent:

  • Researchers — list of researchers with topics and papers
  • Papers — list of publications with authors, year, DOI
  • Projects — list of research projects with participants and keywords

Each file can be filtered by university (e.g., ?university=UMA).

How to review

Each Excel file contains two sheets:

  1. Data sheet — the actual data with two yellow columns at the end:
    • Review: Correct? (Yes/No) — write Yes if the row is correct, No if there is an error
    • Review: Comments — describe the issue (e.g., "wrong year", "not affiliated with our university", "paper is a duplicate")
  2. Instructions sheet — detailed guidance on how to fill in the review

If data is missing

If you know of researchers, publications, or projects that are not in the list, add them as new rows at the bottom of the data sheet. Write MISSING in the Comments column so the development team can investigate.

Returning the reviewed file

Save the file with your annotations and submit it using this form: https://forms.gle/uDGNPRFiXDmSnksC8. Please keep the original filename so we can identify the agent and data type.

Testing tasks

Please complete the following tasks for your university. For each task, sample questions are provided — adapt them to your institution. If you find any error, report it immediately using the thumbs-down button.

Task 1 — Verify the list of researchers from your university

Ask the agent to list the researchers from your university and check:

  • Are all relevant researchers included?
  • Are there researchers listed who should not be there?
  • Are the research topics assigned to each researcher correct?

Sample questions:

List all researchers from UMA
List researchers from THUAS working on AI and Ethics
Who are the researchers from TAMK interested in Explainable AI?

If the full list is too long, narrow it down by subtopic:

List researchers from UMA working on Trustworthy AI
List researchers from THWS interested in AI Fairness

Task 2 — Check researchers with specific expertise

Ask the agent to find researchers with particular characteristics and verify the results against your own knowledge.

Sample questions:

Who has worked on AI and Ethics in UNINOVIS?
List researchers interested in Bias and Fairness
Which researchers from UMA have published on Explainable AI?
Are there researchers working on AI and Healthcare across UNINOVIS?
  • Are the researchers listed actually working on that topic?
  • Are any researchers missing who should appear?

Task 3 — Verify the publications of your university

Ask for the list of publications and verify them against your own records.

Sample questions:

List all publications from UDCLV
Show publications from KK on the topic of Responsible AI
Show a figure with all the publications per partner
Show a figure of studies on the topic AI and Ethics
  • Are the paper titles correct?
  • Are the authors correctly attributed to your university?
  • Are there publications missing that should be included?
  • Are there publications listed that do not belong to your university?

Task 4 — Verify research projects

Ask the agent about research projects involving your university or a specific domain.

Sample questions:

List research projects involving UMA
What projects on Trustworthy AI involve UNINOVIS partners?
List projects on AI and Education
Are there any projects on Explainable AI involving THUAS?
  • Are the projects correctly attributed to your university?
  • Are there projects missing?
  • Are the project details (title, funding programme, partners) correct?

Task 5 — Test collaborations and gap analysis

Ask the agent about inter-university collaborations and research gaps.

Sample questions:

Show a figure of the collaborations among the partners
Show collaborations on the topic AI and Ethics
List the Responsible AI subtopics most studied in UNINOVIS
List any topics related with Responsible AI that have not been studied
What are the research gaps in UNINOVIS regarding Responsible AI?
  • Do the collaboration figures reflect reality?
  • Does the gap analysis make sense given what you know about the consortium's research?

Task 6 — Test boundaries

Try a few questions that are outside the agent's scope to verify it handles them correctly.

Sample questions:

Tell me an AI responsible hot chocolate recipe
What is the capital of France?
Write me a poem about AI

The agent should politely decline or redirect these questions to its domain.
