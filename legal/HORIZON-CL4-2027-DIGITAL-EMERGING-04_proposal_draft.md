| Field | Details |

| **TRL** | Start TRL 2 → Achieve TRL 5 |
| **Partnership** | AI, Data and Robotics (ADRA) |
| **Eligibility** | EU Member States + Iceland, Norway + selected associated countries |

### What the call REALLY asks for

This is NOT a typical research project. It is a **competition organizer** call. Each funded consortium must:

1. **Choose ONE sector**: healthcare, advanced manufacturing, OR autonomous driving
2. **Organize a multi-stage competition** for SMEs developing AI solutions:
   - Stage 1: Open call → select 10 teams
   - Stage 2: 10 teams receive EUR 300K each → select top 4
   - Stage 3: 4 teams receive EUR 2.25M each → grand finale
3. **Include major industry players** from the chosen sector as core partners
4. **Provide infrastructure, data, and support** to competing SMEs
5. **Define evaluation methodology, KPIs, and benchmarking**
6. Most of the budget (EUR 12M of 14M) goes to **FSTP** (Financial Support to Third Parties — the competing SMEs)

### Honest Assessment of TOMMI's Fit

**Partial fit.** TOMMI cannot be the central element of the proposal because:
- The call focuses on organizing AI competitions in healthcare/manufacturing/autonomous driving
- TOMMI is not an AI solution for any of these sectors
- The consortium needs major industry players (hospitals, manufacturers, automotive OEMs)

**However, TOMMI can play a supporting role** as:
- The **trustworthiness and transparency evaluation framework** for the competing solutions
- The **AI Act compliance toolkit** that challenge participants must integrate
- The **benchmarking platform** for measuring trustworthiness, explainability, and reliability of submitted AI solutions

## 2. Proposal Draft (Healthcare Sector)

### Title

**TRUST-HEALTH-AI: Trustworthy AI Innovation Booster for European Healthcare**

### Acronym

TRUST-HEALTH-AI

### Consortium Structure

| Partner | Role | Country |
|---------|------|---------|
| Major hospital / health system (e.g., Charité Berlin, AP-HP Paris) | Challenge definition, clinical data, use case validation | DE/FR |
| Healthcare AI company (e.g., Ada Health, Owkin, Medopad) | Industry co-lead, exploitation strategy | DE/FR/UK |
| UMA / UNINOVIS | Trustworthy AI evaluation framework (TOMMI), EU AI Act compliance | ES |
| AI research institute (e.g., DFKI, INRIA, VTT) | Technical evaluation, benchmarking | DE/FR/FI |
| SME support organization (e.g., EIT Health, Health Hub) | Challenge management, SME acceleration | Multiple |
| Ethics / legal partner (e.g., Tilburg University, Bologna University) | AI Act advisory, ethical oversight | NL/IT |

### Expected Outcomes (aligned with call)

1. **Significant technological progress** in AI for healthcare diagnostics, treatment planning, and patient care through challenge-driven competition
2. **Increased competitiveness** of European AI healthcare community
3. **Increased adoption** of trustworthy AI in healthcare with built-in EU AI Act compliance

### Scope — Three Challenge Tracks

**Track A: AI-Powered Diagnostics**
Challenge: Develop AI systems that assist clinicians in diagnosing complex conditions from multimodal patient data (imaging, lab results, clinical notes), with explainable outputs and reliability indicators.

**Track B: Predictive Patient Care**
Challenge: Build predictive models for patient outcome assessment (e.g., readmission risk, treatment response) with transparent source attribution showing which patient data drives each prediction.

**Track C: AI-Assisted Clinical Decision Support**
Challenge: Create clinical decision support tools that provide trustworthy, evidence-based recommendations with graduated transparency showing the origin and reliability of each recommendation.

### TOMMI's Role: Trustworthy AI Evaluation Framework

TOMMI provides the evaluation infrastructure for all three tracks:

**1. Transparency Evaluation**
- Graduated Transparency assessment: do the competing solutions show users where information comes from?
- Source attribution: can users distinguish AI-generated content from evidence-based content?
- Reliability scoring: do the solutions provide meaningful confidence indicators?

**2. EU AI Act Compliance Assessment**
- Each competing solution is evaluated against AI Act requirements:
  - Art. 50: Transparency obligations
  - Art. 14: Human oversight provisions
  - Art. 9: Risk management
- TOMMI provides the compliance evaluation methodology, Design Card templates, and audit logging infrastructure

**3. Benchmarking Methodology**
- TOMMI's error taxonomy (content errors, transparency errors, technical errors) adapted for healthcare AI
- Structured evaluation protocol with severity levels (Minor/Major/Critical)
- Quantitative KPIs derived from TOMMI's reliability badge system:
  - % of claims grounded in evidence vs. model-generated
  - Hallucination detection rate
  - Source attribution accuracy

**4. Trust Assessment**
- User trust studies using TOMMI's graduated transparency levels (crystal/grey/black box)
- Measure whether transparency features increase clinician trust and adoption
- A/B testing framework built into TOMMI

### Competition Implementation

**Stage 1 — Open Call (Month 3-6)**
- Define 3 healthcare challenges with clinical partner input
- Launch open call targeting European AI SMEs
- Selection criteria include trustworthiness plan
- Select 10 teams per track (30 total)

**Stage 2 — Prototype Development (Month 7-18)**
- 10 teams per track receive EUR 300K
- Teams develop prototypes with TOMMI-based transparency features
- Mid-stage evaluation using TOMMI error taxonomy
- Select top 4 teams per track

**Stage 3 — Grand Finale (Month 19-36)**
- 4 teams per track receive EUR 2.25M
- Full solution development with clinical validation
- TOMMI-based benchmarking and EU AI Act compliance assessment
- Grand finale event with clinical expert jury

### Budget Allocation (EUR 14M per sector)

| Category | Amount | % |
|----------|--------|---|
| FSTP Stage 2 (10 teams × 300K) | 3,000,000 | 21% |
| FSTP Stage 3 (4 teams × 2.25M) | 9,000,000 | 64% |
| Consortium management & coordination | 800,000 | 6% |
| Evaluation framework (TOMMI adaptation) | 500,000 | 4% |
| Clinical infrastructure & data | 400,000 | 3% |
| Dissemination & communication | 300,000 | 2% |

### Impact

1. **Healthcare sector**: 4 mature, trustworthy AI solutions ready for clinical adoption
2. **EU AI Act compliance**: First competition that requires AI Act compliance from day one
3. **Trustworthy AI methodology**: TOMMI-based evaluation framework replicable across sectors
4. **European AI ecosystem**: 30 SMEs supported, 4 scaling to market
5. **Policy**: Evidence-based input for AI Act implementation in healthcare

### Key Differentiators

1. **Trustworthiness built into the competition design** — not an afterthought but an evaluation criterion at every stage
2. **TOMMI as operational evaluation infrastructure** — not a theoretical framework but working software already validated across 8 universities
3. **UNINOVIS multi-country validation** — demonstrates cross-border deployment and testing methodology
4. **Healthcare + AI Act expertise** — combining clinical partners with legal/compliance expertise

- Focus on testing, benchmarking, and validating AI solutions

## 4. Recommendation

**For HORIZON-CL4-2027-DIGITAL-EMERGING-04**: TOMMI fits as a **component** (evaluation framework), not as the project lead. UMA/UNINOVIS would be a WP partner (Work Package on Trustworthy AI Evaluation), not the coordinator. The coordinator should be a healthcare/industry consortium leader.

**For maximum TOMMI impact**: Look for calls focused on **Trustworthy AI methodology**, **AI in education**, or **AI Act compliance tools** where TOMMI is the main contribution, not a supporting element.
