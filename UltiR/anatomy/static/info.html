🦴 Anatomy Explorer

Interactive human anatomy — explore and test yourself on muscles and bones

What this tool does

Anatomy Explorer shows clickable diagrams of the human body inside Moodle. Students switch between muscles and bones and between the anterior and posterior views, explore each structure, and practise with quizzes.

  • About 50 muscles and 25 bones or bone groups, organised by region (head & neck, trunk, upper limb, lower limb)
  • Spanish and English interface and names, with the Latin term (Terminologia Anatomica) for every structure
  • Left and right are always the patient's sides, as marked under the diagram
  • No LLM and nothing to install — everything runs in the browser

For students

  1. Explore: hover over the diagram (or tap it) to see each structure's name; pick one from the list to locate it. Your teacher may have added notes.
  2. Quiz: choose a region and start. Questions are either "click this structure on the diagram" or "which structure is highlighted?" (four options; keys 1–4 work too).
  3. After a wrong answer the correct structure is shown in green and the tool tells you what you clicked. At the end you can retry only the ones you missed.
Practice mode: quiz results are not stored and are not sent to the Moodle gradebook.

For teachers

Launch the tool from your course and click ⚙ Settings. For each course you can:

  • Turn muscles and/or bones on or off, and choose which regions are included
  • Tick or untick individual structures (unticked ones are shown faded and are never asked)
  • Set the number of quiz questions and the question types allowed
  • Ask for a specific side ("left deltoid") instead of accepting either side
  • Add a timer, either per question or for the whole quiz

To add a note to a structure, select it in Explore mode and type in the box under the diagram. Students see the note when they select that structure.
