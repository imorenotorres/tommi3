🎯 Bloom's Taxonomy Trainer

Interactive exercises on Bloom's revised taxonomy — classify, match and design learning objectives

What this tool does

Bloom's Taxonomy Trainer helps students of education develop a working command of Bloom's revised taxonomy (Anderson & Krathwohl, 2001). An interactive pyramid serves as a permanent reference, and three exercise modes build progressively deeper understanding.

6 · Create
5 · Evaluate
4 · Analyze
3 · Apply
2 · Understand
1 · Remember

🎯 Classify

Given a complete learning objective with its key verb highlighted, identify the Bloom's level. Then confirm with a matching verb.

🔍 Verb Match

Given a Bloom's verb, identify its level. Then select the learning objective that reflects the same cognitive operation.

🚀 Design

Select the objective that matches a given level. Then escalate: identify the objective at the adjacent level.

All exercises are randomly generated from a bank of 60+ authentic objectives across all six levels. Answers are checked server-side — no LLM involved.

For students

  1. Open the Pyramid tab — click any level to see its description, key verbs and example objectives.
  2. Choose an exercise type from the Exercises tab.
  3. Click the coloured level buttons or A/B/C/D options to answer each step.
  4. Use Hint if you're unsure — it shows relevant verbs without revealing the answer.
  5. After completing a step you see the explanation; after all steps you get a score.
The pyramid is always available in the Pyramid tab as a reference — you can switch tabs at any time.
