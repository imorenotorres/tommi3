Circuit types

Series circuits

2-4 resistors in series. Calculate total resistance, current, voltage drops across each resistor, and total power.

Parallel circuits

2-3 resistors in parallel. Calculate equivalent resistance, total current, branch currents, and total power.

Series-parallel combinations

One resistor in series with a parallel pair. Requires breaking the circuit into sub-problems: first the parallel equivalent, then the series total.

Features

  • Step-by-step — each calculation is checked individually, not just the final answer
  • SVG circuit diagrams — visual representation of the circuit with component values
  • Hints — each step has a hint showing the relevant formula
  • Tolerant grading — accepts rounding differences (2% tolerance)
  • Random values — uses realistic E12-series resistor values and standard supply voltages
  • No AI — all computations are deterministic
  • Unlimited practice — generate as many exercises as you want
