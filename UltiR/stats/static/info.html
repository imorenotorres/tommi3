📈 Statistics Explorer

Step-by-step statistics exercises and interactive distribution visualizer

What this tool does

Statistics Explorer gives students guided, auto-graded exercises on the core topics of descriptive and inferential statistics — directly inside Moodle. It also includes an interactive normal distribution visualizer to build intuition about probability and parameters.

📊 Descriptive Statistics

Given a dataset, calculate mean, median, population variance and standard deviation step by step.

📏 Confidence Intervals

Construct a z-based confidence interval (90%, 95%, 99%) for a population mean with σ known.

🧮 Z-Test

One-sample two-tailed z-test: compute the test statistic, find the critical value and decide.

👀 Distribution Explorer

Interactive bell curve: adjust μ and σ with sliders and compute P(X ≤ x) with visual shading.

All exercises are randomly generated from a large parameter space — no two sessions are identical. Answers are verified server-side with a 2% tolerance (no rounding penalties).

For students

  1. Open the tool from your Moodle course (it launches automatically).
  2. Choose an exercise type from the tabs at the top.
  3. Work through each step, entering your numeric answer and pressing Check.
  4. Use the Hint button if you get stuck — it shows the formula without the answer.
  5. At the end you get a score and can request a new exercise.
  6. In the Explorer tab, use the sliders to experiment with the normal distribution.
The Z-Test "reject" step accepts 1 (reject H₀) or 0 (fail to reject). A dropdown is provided — no need to type.
