Writing: Project

AI-assisted writing projects with documented decision-making

An LTI tool that guides students through structured research or writing projects with AI assistance. Every interaction is recorded, and students must explicitly accept, adapt or reject each AI suggestion with a written justification.

For instructors

Course setup

  • Upload reference documents (PDF, Markdown, text) — the AI will ground its responses in these materials (RAG)
  • Configure project phases — default is Introduction / Methodology / Results / Discussion, but you can define your own
  • Customise the system prompt — adapt the AI's behaviour to your discipline and pedagogical approach

Review and assessment

  • See all student projects and their full interaction history
  • Score each interaction (0-4) based on the quality of the student's decision and justification
  • Export all interactions as TSV (compatible with Excel)

For students

How it works

  • Create a project and invite group members
  • Work through each phase — ask the AI for guidance, drafts or feedback
  • For every AI response, you must decide: use it, adapt it, or reject it — and explain why
  • Your decisions and justifications are visible to the instructor and form part of your assessment

Key features

  • RAG-grounded responses — the AI answers based on course materials, not just general knowledge
  • Decision tracking — every accept/reject/adapt is logged with a mandatory justification
  • Per-course isolation — each Moodle course has its own documents, configuration and projects
  • No extra login — students access directly from Moodle via LTI
Pedagogical rationale: The value of this tool is not the AI's output — it is the student's documented reasoning about that output. By requiring explicit decisions with justifications, the tool develops critical thinking and makes the learning process visible and assessable.
