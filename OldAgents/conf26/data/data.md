# UNINOVIS Conference 2026 and DiPYUA Workshop Data

This document contains information about the UNINOVIS Alliance, UNINOVIS Conference 2026 and DiPYUA Workshop, including authors, participants, platforms, scientific programs, and social events. The social events are the same for the Conference and the Workshop. It also includes the abstracts of all the Conference presentations.

## UNINOVIS Alliance

UniNovis is a European university alliance focused on enhancing education, research, and innovation in the field of applied data science. UniNovis consists of eigth European universities from eight different countries:

| University                                    | Acronym    | Country     
| ----------------------------------------------| ---------- | -----------

Beyond research, SMBH promotes innovation through data-driven education and e-learning platforms for healthcare professionals, emphasizing data quality, sharing, and ethical regulation.

This presentation aims to foster new opportunities for collaboration with UNINOVIS partners, strengthening joint initiatives at the intersection of health, social sciences, and data science.

Four laboratories specialize in large-scale data analysis. EREN, a pioneer in public health data, developed the European Nutri-Score and manages extensive cohort studies. LEPS designs evidence-based health education programs through patient survey analytics. IRIS explores the sociological dimensions of health using social data mining, while LIMICS focuses on medical informatics, statistical modelling, decision support systems, and data infrastructures. Moreover, hospital-affiliated teams handle clinical data, from imaging and DNA sequencing to patient record integration, contributing to the shared Paris public hospital database.

Beyond research, SMBH fosters innovation through data-driven education and e-learning platforms for health professionals, emphasizing data management, quality, sharing, and regulation.

This presentation aims to open new opportunities for cooperation with UNINOVIS partners, reinforcing joint initiatives at the interface of health, technology, and data science.
